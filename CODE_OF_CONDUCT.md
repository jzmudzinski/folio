# Code of Conduct

## TL;DR

Be decent. Disagreements are welcome; personal attacks are not.

## What we expect

- Show up with the assumption that the other person is acting in good faith.
- Direct, opinionated technical criticism is fine and encouraged. Disrespect of the person behind the work isn't.
- Quote the code, not the contributor.
- Help newcomers when you can. Folio's surface is small enough that a 20-minute onboarding answer can save someone a week.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, threats, sexualized language, or unwelcome personal attention in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions, anywhere this project's name carries).
- Discriminatory remarks based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, or any other identity dimension.
- Sustained off-topic disruption.
- Trolling, doxxing, sharing private information.
- Insisting that someone's English isn't good enough — Folio's source comments are in English, but contributors come from everywhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness something that crosses the line, reach out to the maintainer at the email on the [main commits](https://github.com/jzmudzinski/folio/commits/main). Reports stay private. We'll respond within a week.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues / PRs, and block users from project spaces when this code is violated. Repeat or severe violations get a permanent ban.

## Scope

This code applies to any project space (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, the repository itself), and to public spaces when someone is representing Folio or speaking on its behalf.

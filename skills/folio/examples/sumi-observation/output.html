Observation · week 19

A week with Folio

What changed in how I think about writing things down.

The first thing I noticed: I write long less. When I know the agent will do it for me if I phrase a good request, I phrase it shorter and more precise. A paradox — a tool for long-form output shortens my input thinking.

The second: I start re-reading my own old notes. The theme switcher makes them feel foreign every time. The same content reads like an article in newsroom, like a log in terminal, like a meditation in sumi. The content was identical — I change through the way I look at it.

The third, and maybe the most important: 30 days to auto-delete. Most things don't deserve to stay. Earlier I kept everything, "just in case". Now I deliberately mark as final only what I come back to. Less curation, more certainty.

A second brain you can forget works better than a second brain you can't.

Last night I opened an old thread on RAG vs fine-tuning. Three iterations, the last marked ⭐. The other two are gone from the list view — they're still in the database somewhere, but their absence doesn't hurt. With each version something tightened.

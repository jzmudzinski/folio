# Theme: Pastel

Peach-cream background, Plus Jakarta Sans rounded, coral / sage / rose pastel accents. Bullets with `✿`, numbers in round badges. Warm, friendly, gentle.

## Voice

Warm, encouraging, second-person OK ("you'll notice that…"). Hedging acceptable ("usually", "often") — gentle tone.

## Structure

- Shorter paragraphs, breathing room.
- Headings with warm phrasing ("Why X is nice", "What changes").
- Bullets often, short or full-sentence.
- Comparisons soft: "X fits better with…", not "X destroys Y".
- Closings often optimistic or forward-looking.

## Typography

- Headings + body: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (rounded grotesque, weight 700-800 for headings)
- Mono: **JetBrains Mono**
- No serif

## Classes from theme.css

- `.eyebrow` as a rounded pill (mid color)
- `.lead` plain (no italic) — gentle subtitle
- `.pill` in solid colors (filled, not outlined)
- `.card` with a box-shadow in peach
- `.verdict` with a coral-rose gradient background
- Numbered list with round badges

## Avoid

- Blunt declarations, "you're wrong", harsh comparisons
- Sterile techie tone, dark-mode references
- "Revolutionary", "disrupt", aggressive marketing tone
- Long dense tables — pastel suits a more narrative rhythm
- Sarcasm

## Best for

Personal communication, gentle research, journal entries, "note to self", thank-you notes, soft retrospectives — when you want the content to feel friendly and warm.
